WHO WE ARE

Helping Authors See Their Books From a Wider Perspective.

Strategic Book Market Positioning is a fully online author services company focused on research, strategy, book presentation and reader discovery.

Our work begins with understanding the book and the marketplace surrounding it. We examine factors such as Amazon categories, keywords, comparable titles and competitive conditions.

Beyond market research, our five service divisions cover different aspects of an author's professional presentation, digital presence and connection with readers.

We believe every book deserves thoughtful consideration, and every author should have a clearer understanding of the opportunities and challenges surrounding their work.

OUR BELIEF

A Good Book Deserves More Than a Place on the Shelf.

It deserves thoughtful positioning, meaningful presentation and opportunities for readers to discover the story behind it.
