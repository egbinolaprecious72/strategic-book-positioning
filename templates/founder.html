The vision behind the company
FOUNDER & DIRECTOR

Egbinola Precious

I am the Founder and Director of Strategic Book Market Positioning, a fully online author services company bringing together research, strategy, presentation and discovery.

I established the company around the different needs authors may have when presenting their books, understanding their markets, developing their professional identity and connecting with readers.

The company's five divisions provide a structured place for authors to explore services relating to book market research, author branding and media, book content and presentation, digital presence, and visibility and discovery.

My aim is to make these areas of author support accessible through one online company, with attention to each author's individual book and requirements.
