OUR SERVICE STRUCTURE

Thoughtful Support for Every Part of the Author Journey.

Strategic Book Market Positioning is a fully online author services company. Our work is organised into five divisions, each addressing a different aspect of book research, presentation, author development and reader discovery.

Authors can enquire about an individual service or discuss several areas that relate to their current needs.

DIVISION ONE

Book Market Research & Positioning

We examine a book's subject, genre, comparable titles and intended readership to understand how it fits within its market. This research helps shape clearer positioning and informs decisions about relevant categories, keywords and reader discovery pathways.

01

Amazon Category & Subcategory Research

We examine the categories and subcategories available for a book's subject and genre. The research considers relevance, competing titles and how readers browse related topics, helping identify suitable category pathways.

02

Amazon Keyword Research

We research words and search phrases readers may use when looking for books on a particular subject. The work considers the book's themes, genre, audience and comparable titles to develop a relevant keyword direction.

03

Competitor Analysis

We review comparable books, including their subjects, descriptions, categories and presentation. This provides a clearer picture of how similar titles are positioned and where a book may have distinctive qualities.

04

Strategic Book Market Positioning

We bring together research about the book, its intended readers and its competitive landscape. The resulting positioning direction clarifies the book's place in its market and the qualities that distinguish it.

05

Reader Discoverability Research

We explore how readers interested in a particular subject or genre find and evaluate books. This can include examining search language, reader interests, related topics and relevant discovery pathways.

06

Market Opportunity Analysis

We assess relevant areas within a book's market, considering reader interests, comparable titles and subject-specific opportunities. The findings help identify areas worth exploring in the book's positioning and presentation.

02
DIVISION TWO

Author Branding & Media Services

We organise and develop the professional materials that introduce an author and their work. This includes shaping biographies, profiles, media kits and book portfolios so that an author's background, identity and published work are presented clearly and consistently.

01

Personalised Author Media Kit Creation

We assemble a structured media kit containing selected author information and book details. Depending on the project, it may include a biography, author photograph, book summaries and other materials useful for introducing the author.

02

Author Biography Development

We develop or refine an author's biography using relevant background, writing experience, interests and achievements supplied for the project. The result is a clear introduction suitable for professional author platforms and related materials.

03

Professional Author Profile Presentation

We organise an author's key information into a consistent professional profile. This includes presenting their background, writing focus and published work in a way that is clear and easy for readers or industry contacts to understand.

04

Book Portfolio Preparation

We bring together information about an author's books in an organised portfolio. This can include titles, descriptions, publication details and other supplied information, creating a coherent overview of the author's body of work.

05

Author Brand Presentation

We consider how an author's identity, biography, books and professional materials appear together. The work focuses on consistency in tone, presentation and the way the author's writing identity is communicated.

03
DIVISION THREE

Book Content & Presentation

We review the written and visual materials through which readers encounter a book. From descriptions and blurbs to manuscript formatting and edition presentation, the focus is on clarity, consistency, readability and a well-organised presentation.

01

Book Description Review

We assess the structure, clarity and relevance of a book description. The review considers whether it communicates the subject, central idea, intended readership and key appeal in a way that is easy to follow.

02

Book Blurb Development

We develop or refine concise book copy that introduces the story, subject or central message. The wording is shaped around the book's content and intended readers while keeping its main appeal clear and accurate.

03

Book Presentation Review

We examine the available information and presentation materials associated with a book. This may include its description, author information and edition details, with attention to clarity, consistency and completeness.

04

Manuscript Formatting

We arrange manuscript text into a consistent, readable document based on the agreed format and project requirements. This can include heading styles, paragraph layout, spacing, page structure and other formatting details.

05

Ebook and Paperback Presentation

We review how ebook and paperback editions are presented, including relevant book information and edition-specific materials. The aim is to support a clear and consistent presentation across the formats being prepared.

04
DIVISION FOUR

Author Digital Presence

We help establish and organise an author's online presence through websites, landing pages and digital profile materials. The aim is to create clear online spaces where visitors can learn about the author, explore their books and find relevant information.

01

Author Website Creation

We create a website that introduces the author and provides a clear place to present their books, biography and relevant updates. The structure and page content are planned around the author's identity and the information visitors need.

02

Author Landing Page Creation

We create a focused page for a particular author, book or project. It brings the most relevant information together in one place and gives visitors a clear route to explore the subject further.

03

Author Social Media Profile Setup

We help establish selected professional social media profiles by organising profile information, descriptions and relevant presentation details. The setup is aligned with the author's identity and the platforms selected for the project.

04

Digital Author Portfolio

We prepare an online collection of an author's professional information and book details. It provides an organised way to introduce the author and present selected titles and related materials digitally.

05

Online Author Profile Development

We develop a structured author profile for online use, bringing together relevant biographical information, writing interests and book details. The presentation is designed to make the author's work and identity easy to understand.

05
DIVISION FIVE

Author Visibility & Discovery Strategy

We develop reader-focused approaches that help introduce authors and books to relevant audiences. Depending on the project, this can involve discovery initiatives, outreach, reading campaigns and activities that encourage genuine engagement around books and literary interests.

01

Book Discovery Initiatives

We plan activities that introduce a book to reader groups, literary spaces and audiences with relevant interests. The approach is shaped around the book's subject and the kinds of readers most likely to connect with it.

02

Reader Engagement and Connection

We develop ways to encourage meaningful interaction around a book, its themes and the author's perspective. Activities are planned with the intended readership and the nature of the book in mind.

03

Author-to-Reader Outreach

We plan thoughtful communication that introduces an author and their work to relevant readers or reader communities. The focus is on appropriate audience selection, clear messaging and genuine relevance.

04

Reading Campaigns

We organise reading-focused activities around selected books, themes or reading periods. A campaign may include coordinated reading prompts, discussion opportunities and other activities suited to its audience.

05

Reader Community Development

We explore ways to bring readers with shared literary interests together around books and authors. This can involve planning community activities, discussion spaces and ongoing opportunities for participation.

06

Author and Reader Engagement Activities

We plan activities that create opportunities for authors and readers to interact through literary conversations, book discussions and related experiences. Each activity is considered in relation to the book and its audience.

07

Book Promotion Through Reader Experiences

We explore ways to introduce books through engaging reading experiences and reader participation. Activities are designed around the book's themes and the interests of the intended audience.
