OUR CORE SPECIALITY

Understanding Where Your Book Fits in the Market.

Strategic Book Market Positioning is centred on researching the marketplace surrounding a book. We examine relevant categories, search terms, comparable titles and market opportunities to help authors make more informed decisions about how their books are positioned on Amazon.

  • Amazon category and subcategory research
  • Amazon keyword research
  • Competitor and comparable-book analysis
  • Reader discoverability research
  • Market opportunity analysis
  • Strategic book market positioning

The focus is on research and practical direction based on the individual book, rather than treating every title as if it belongs in the same market.
