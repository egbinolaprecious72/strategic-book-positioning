Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, store and handle personal information.

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Strategic Book Market Positioning respects the privacy of authors, prospective clients and website visitors. This Privacy Policy explains how information provided through our website is collected, used, stored and shared when individuals make enquiries or communicate with us.

1. Information We Collect

When you submit a project enquiry through our Get Started page, we may collect the following information:

  • Your name and email address.
  • Your book title and genre.
  • Your Amazon book link, if provided.
  • Your publishing status.
  • The service divisions and specific services you select.
  • Your project goals, requirements and other details included in your enquiry.

We may also receive additional information if you voluntarily provide it when replying to our emails or communicating with us.

Our Get Started form does not currently provide direct file upload fields for professional photographs or book covers. Please do not include confidential or sensitive information in your enquiry unless it is necessary for your request.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use submitted information for purposes such as:

  • Receiving and responding to project enquiries.
  • Understanding your book and publishing requirements.
  • Reviewing your selected service interests and project goals.
  • Assessing relevant book market research and positioning requirements.
  • Communicating about possible services, proposals and next steps.
  • Managing client enquiries and maintaining relevant business records.
  • Maintaining and improving our website and business operations.

3. How Enquiry Information Is Stored

Information submitted through the Get Started form is recorded in a database used to manage and review enquiries. Access to enquiry records through the website's administration area is restricted to authorised administration access.

We take reasonable steps to protect stored information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure or loss. However, no method of electronic storage or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

We do not sell personal information submitted through our website.

4. Email Communication and Google Services

We use Gmail-compatible email services to send business notifications and communicate with individuals who submit enquiries.

When an enquiry is submitted:

  • An email notification containing the submitted enquiry details is sent to our designated business notification address.
  • A confirmation email is sent to the email address provided by the author.

Relevant information, including contact details and enquiry content, is therefore processed through the email service used to send these messages. Google's handling of information is also subject to its own privacy policies and terms.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent personal information. Information may be processed by service providers where necessary to operate our website, store enquiry records or send email communications.

Information may also be disclosed where required by law, through a valid legal process, or where reasonably necessary to protect our rights, website or legitimate interests.

6. Cookies and Website Functionality

Our website uses technical functionality necessary for its operation. Certain features, such as administrator login sessions and security protections, may rely on browser storage or cookies.

This policy does not claim that advertising or analytics tracking is being used. If additional tracking technologies are introduced, this policy will be reviewed and updated to explain their use.

7. External Websites

Our website may contain links to external websites and platforms, including Amazon and social media services. These external platforms operate independently and have their own privacy practices. We are not responsible for how they collect or use information.

We encourage visitors to review the privacy policies of external websites before sharing personal information with them.

8. How Long We Keep Information

Enquiry information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to requests, manage business relationships, maintain appropriate records and address legal or administrative obligations.

Where information is no longer required for these purposes, we will consider appropriate deletion or secure disposal, subject to applicable legal requirements.

9. Your Privacy Choices and Requests

You may contact us to ask whether we hold personal information you have submitted. Subject to applicable law, you may also request access to your information, correction of inaccurate details, or deletion of information where deletion is appropriate.

You may also choose not to submit an enquiry. However, information such as your name, email address and project details is necessary for us to review and respond to a project request.

We will consider privacy requests in accordance with applicable data protection requirements.

10. Children's Privacy

Our website and professional services are intended for individuals conducting author, publishing or related professional activities. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children.

If you believe a child has submitted personal information to us, please contact us so that the matter can be reviewed.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website, services, technology or information-handling practices change. The revised policy will be published on this page with an updated date.
